---

## Exam Overview

The Certified Blockchain Business Foundations (CBBF) certification validates foundational knowledge of blockchain technology, its operating principles, business suitability, and potential organizational applications.

- **Exam Format**: Online multiple-choice and true/false examination
- **Target Audience**: Business managers, IT leaders, business analysts, project managers, IT consultants, and other professionals evaluating or supporting blockchain initiatives
- **Prerequisites**: No formal prerequisites are specified on the official BTA exam page

---

## Core Exam Domains & Knowledge Areas

### 1. General Blockchain Knowledge

- Blockchain fundamentals, distributed ledgers, and the purpose of shared records.
- Core terminology, network participants, blocks, transactions, and ledger structure.
- Public and private blockchain concepts and common business contexts.

### 2. Why Use Blockchain

- Business problems suited to shared, tamper-evident records and multi-party coordination.
- Potential benefits such as transparency, traceability, and reduced reliance on a central intermediary.
- Limitations and trade-offs, including governance, privacy, performance, cost, and when a conventional database is more appropriate.

### 3. How Blockchain Works

- How transactions are grouped into blocks and linked using cryptographic techniques.
- Distributed networks, nodes, consensus mechanisms, and transaction validation.
- Smart contracts and the role of automation in blockchain-based workflows.

### 4. Using Blockchain for Business

- Identifying and evaluating business use cases across organizations and industries.
- Planning implementation around stakeholders, processes, governance, and technology choices.
- Understanding adoption considerations such as integration, security, scalability, privacy, and operational readiness.
